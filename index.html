About

My name is Matthew, and I am a 2029 graduate and midfielder with Westhill High School and Syracuse Development Academy. Soccer has been my passion for as long as I can remember, and my goal is to compete at the collegiate level where I can continue growing as both a player and a person.

On the field, I pride myself on being highly coachable, resilient, and a hard worker. I show up every day ready to learn, push through challenges, and give everything I have for my team. I believe those qualities are what separate good players from great ones.
